# FastrBuild visual system

**Approved direction:** Sea glass, Newsreader, `fastrBuild.` wordmark. Grounded in the founder's selected direction and the public site released 27 September 2026. Use these rules across media without copying the website composition wholesale.

## 1. Identity and voice

- **Visual wordmark:** `fastrBuild.` — lowercase `fastr`, uppercase `B`, lowercase `uild`, final period. Set in Plus Jakarta Sans ExtraBold (800), with tight tracking (about `-0.045em`). On light backgrounds, use deep teal letters and a dark coral period. On a dark background, use warm white letters and a visible peach period. Keep it as text or accurately typeset vector text; no separate icon has been selected.
- **Prose name:** FastrBuild. Avoid `fastBuild`, `fastrbuild`, or `Fastrbuild` in running copy unless quoting an external proper name or URL.
- **Personality:** warm, capable, direct, positive. Express the human advantage in real work rather than generic AI spectacle. The strategic category is “forward-deployed AI team”; “Build a Super Company” is the emotional destination. Use `$fastrbuild-context` for the complete, current message and claim boundaries.

## 2. Core palette

| Role | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Deep teal / ink | `#174D52` | Main text, dark fields, outlines |
| Mint paper | `#F3FAF6` | Main light background |
| Warm white | `#FFFEFA` | Cards, clean content areas, reverse text |
| Seafoam | `#A7DAD1` | Larger supporting field, Super Company section |
| Peach | `#F4A88E` | Primary action and warm accent |
| Muted teal | `#3D6768` | Secondary text on light backgrounds |
| Dark coral | `#9B4D3C` | Small accent text |

Supporting website colors may be used when the composition needs them: dark coral `#B96350` for the wordmark period and small marks on light backgrounds; warm sand `#F9F5ED` for reading sections; soft peach `#F3C6AE` for a large commercial field; deeper teal `#214D50` for dark information sections. Keep the core four colors (teal, mint, seafoam, peach) recognizable.

**Readable pairs:** deep teal on mint, warm white, seafoam, and peach; warm white on deep teal; muted teal or dark coral on mint. Do not put white text on peach or use peach as paragraph text on a light field. Use peach to attract attention, then use deep teal for the words inside a peach button. The measured contrast of deep teal on mint is about 8.9:1; deep teal on seafoam about 6.1:1; deep teal on peach about 4.9:1.

## 3. Type and hierarchy

- **Display:** Newsreader Regular (400), with Italic for a deliberate emphasis within a headline. Georgia is the fallback. Let its shapes and whitespace carry expression; avoid decorative effects or heavy shadows.
- **Body, controls, labels, wordmark:** Plus Jakarta Sans, generally 400–600 for reading and 700–800 for navigation, labels, and the wordmark. Arial is the fallback.
- **Website reference:** hero display roughly `clamp(56px, 5.8vw, 91px)` at `1.02` line height; section titles roughly `clamp(50px, 5.9vw, 86px)` at `1.04`; body copy 16px with spacious line height. Adapt scale to the medium instead of importing these sizes into a slide or document.
- Use short, prominent headings and comfortable body measure. Small uppercase labels can orient the reader, but should not carry paragraphs. Newsreader can set a headline or key result; Plus Jakarta carries detail.

## 4. Composition and components

- Favor generous margins, a clear reading order, large editorial type, restrained color blocking, and fine teal separators. Let a section breathe before introducing another information layer.
- Use warm white or mint cards with subtle borders and corners around 12–13px when cards clarify a comparison. Cards are a tool, not the default treatment for every fact.
- Actions can be pill shaped. The live site uses a peach primary button with deep teal text and a teal-outline secondary button, each at least about 52px high. Preserve visible focus states.
- A large word such as **SUPER** can sit behind a seafoam section as low-priority decoration. The approved website uses deep teal at about 20% opacity, visibly stronger than the first faint version while keeping foreground text dominant.
- For long lists, establish the choice or takeaway first. Disclosure controls can hold secondary detail when appropriate. This is a general readability principle; the proposed redesign of the current three offer cards has **not** been approved or shipped.

## 5. Imagery and motion

- Use imagery only when it advances understanding or emotional connection. The current site pairs its hero copy with an illustrative, candid human collaboration scene and an overlapping operational summary card; later sections are image-free.
- Favor ordinary work, real materials, attentive gestures, soft daylight, and human-scale settings. Avoid robots, holograms, neon, fabricated interfaces, generic corporate poses, and made-up client proof.
- AI-generated or staged scenes need an unobtrusive visible “Illustrative scene” label and descriptive alternative text when they could be mistaken for a real team, client, or engagement.
- Keep interaction quiet: slight button lift, soft surface changes, and gentle image movement only where useful. The site uses roughly 200–300ms control transitions and a slower hero image hover. Honor `prefers-reduced-motion`; never make motion necessary to read content.

## 6. Adapt by medium

- **Web and product UI:** use the palette as semantic roles, clear hierarchy, readable content density, focus visibility, and responsive layouts. Operational interfaces should prioritize task clarity over the website's editorial scale.
- **Slides and social graphics:** use one idea per frame, Newsreader for the central thought, Plus Jakarta Sans for evidence, and one main color field. Keep the wordmark modest and legible.
- **Documents and proposals:** use the wordmark, deep teal headings, warm light backgrounds, and restrained peach highlights. Reduce decorative scale for long-form reading and print.
- **Co-branded or client-owned work:** follow the client's identity as the primary system. Apply FastrBuild identity only to the clearly identified FastrBuild contribution.

## 7. Decision status

**Approved and shipped:** Sea glass palette; Newsreader + Plus Jakarta Sans; `fastrBuild.` wordmark with accent period; no icon; illustrative hero image; restrained interactions; stronger SUPER background lettering.

**Explored, not selected:** Instrument Serif heading direction, signal-lime visual direction, standalone icon concepts, additional illustrative images throughout the site.

**Proposed, not shipped:** shortening the three offer cards through progressive disclosure. Treat it as a future design option, not a requirement.
