## Remaining limits

No reliable speaker-labelled spontaneous speech was found in this review. An available local automated Malayalam-to-English translation was excluded because both speaker identity and wording were unreliable. The older local site's two essay files have unknown authorship and differ from the current live archive, so they were not used as main anchors.

No exhaustive crawl of private GitHub, Coda, messages, email or every local project was performed. The Notes inventory is a title-level survey; most individual pages were not read. No independent cross-model voice-fidelity test has been run. These limitations constrain confidence, not the ability to use and improve this first version.
