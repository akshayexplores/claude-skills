# Happy Customer Interview — Field Guide

*Print this. Keep it in front of you on the call.*

---

## Before

- [ ] 20 minutes booked
- [ ] Recording ready
- [ ] You are **not** this account's owner
- [ ] You have read nothing about their open issues — you want to be genuinely curious

---

## The ask (when booking)

> "I'd love twenty minutes of your time — I'm interested in your thoughts and experiences working with us. Would that work?"

---

## Opening

> "Thanks for the time. Is it okay with you if I record this?"

---

## THE RULE

**You talk 5%. They talk 95%.**

If you're explaining, defending, or clarifying — stop. Ask the next question instead.

---

## Questions, in order

**1.** What are some of your favourite things about working with us?

**2.** **How does that help you?** ← *the most important question on this call*

**3.** Follow everything with:
> *Why?* · *How?* · *What else?* · *Tell me more.* · *Why do you say that?*
> *Really?* · *That's interesting.*

**4.** How many suppliers, providers or consultants do you work with?

**5.** If you ranked them best to worst on the things we've been talking about — *[say their own words back]* — where would you rank us?

**6.** Interesting. Is there anything else that puts us there?

**7.** Tell me how you view your relationship with Acsia. What's it like working with us?

**8.** Give me three descriptive words that come to mind when you think about working with us.

---

## DO NOT ASK

### ❌ "What could we do better?"

You will want to. It feels responsible. **Don't.**

It turns an appreciation call into a critique call, and a critique recording does the opposite of what this exercise is for. There are other places to collect that.

If they volunteer a criticism: acknowledge briefly, note it, steer back. Don't chase it.

---

## Listen for these — write them down word for word

- 🎯 **"I didn't know you did that."**
- 🎯 **"We give that to [competitor]."**
- 🎯 Anything about *how it helped* rather than *what was delivered*
- 🎯 Any three-word description you could put on a slide

These are the clips that change the team's mind. Timestamp them.

---

## Close

> "Thank you so much — this was really valuable. I appreciate it."

---

## After (same day, 5 minutes)

- [ ] Timestamp the 2–3 best moments
- [ ] Pull direct quotes into the testimonial bank
- [ ] Log any "didn't know you did that" into the cross-sell map
- [ ] Cut a 30–90 second clip for the Monday huddle
