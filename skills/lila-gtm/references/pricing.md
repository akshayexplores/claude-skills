# Pricing

Source: LiLa Pitch Deck v5.1, slide 19 (14 Oct 2026). This replaces the May 2026 project brief pricing.

**Before quoting a price externally, confirm it's still current with Acsia.** Pricing on a pitch deck is not always the published price list.

## SaaS - credit-based subscription

| Plan | Monthly | Annual | Credits per month |
|---|---|---|---|
| Professional | $499 | $4,990 | 2,000 |
| Business | $1,299 | $12,990 | 4,000 |
| Project | $3,699 | $36,990 | 16,000 |

- Unused credits don't carry forward.
- Add-on credits available on all plans. On annual plans, add-ons are limited to the plan duration.
- Annual pricing works out to roughly 10 months' price for 12 months.
- **Best for:** Professional for pilots and single teams; Business for mid-size programs; Project for large Tier-1 programs.

## On-prem perpetual licence

- **$100,000 per licence**, unlimited usage for the purchased workflow.
- Annual maintenance (AMC) available at additional cost - amount `[VERIFY with Acsia]`.
- Runs fully offline on the customer's own infrastructure.
- **Best for:** OEMs, large Tier-1s, defence and aerospace, and anyone who can't use cloud SaaS for IP reasons.

## Superseded - do not quote

From the May 2026 project brief: Starter tier ($299), Business at $1,099 / 5,000 credits, Project at 16,667 credits, on-prem at $260,000 first year / $167,000 per additional product, $0.05 overage per credit. If someone asks for these, give the current figures and flag the change.

## ROI framing

- Allowed: "Modelled at 15-25x return in the first year across SaaS plans." Always "modelled". INTERNAL use only (sales conversations, proposals), not ads or public pages.
- Better: build the ROI from the prospect's own numbers. Engineer hours per program on test cases or requirements, times loaded hourly cost, times the cleared saving for that agent (e.g. 98% on test case creation). Show the working.
- What a credit buys isn't documented in the sources. Don't estimate credits-per-task. `[VERIFY with Acsia]` if a prospect asks.

## Which plan to suggest

| Situation | Suggest |
|---|---|
| First conversation, wants to try it | Proof challenge, then Professional |
| One team, one program | Professional or Business |
| Several programs, large Tier-1 | Project |
| IP can't leave the network, or defence/aerospace | On-prem |
