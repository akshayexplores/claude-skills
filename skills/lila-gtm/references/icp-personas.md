# ICP, personas and buying signals

## Who to target, by motion

### Product motion (LiLA sold as SaaS or on-prem)

Phasing from Deck v5.1:

| Phase | Who | Where |
|---|---|---|
| 2026 | Tier-2s and engineering service providers (ESPs), smaller teams and pilots, companies under active ASPICE mandates | US, EU/UK, India |
| 2027-28 | Growing Tier-1s and Tier-2s, mid-size to large programs | + Japan, Australia, Canada |
| 2029-30 | OEMs, large Tier-1s, large defence and aerospace programs | + Middle East, global |

Best-fit company today: a Tier-2 supplier or ESP doing embedded or automotive software, under OEM pressure to reach ASPICE Level 2 or 3, running several programs at once with a stretched test team.

### Services-wedge motion (LiLA opens an Acsia engineering deal)

From the FY27 Revenue Engine plan:

1. **Tier-1 suppliers - new-logo priority.** 500-20,000 employees, doing cockpit/IVI, BMS/e-mobility or telematics software. Buy in 3-6 months, more open to new vendors than OEMs.
2. **Existing Acsia accounts - expansion.** Hand off to the `outgrow-acsia` skill.
3. **OEMs - relationship motion only.** GM, Stellantis, Volvo, JLR, Hyundai/Kia EU, EV-native players. Worked by the Sales Head through events and referrals, never by campaign sequence.
4. **System integrators / engineering partners** - opportunistic only.

Deprioritised: hardware/PCB-only work, automotive startups with under 2 years of runway, non-automotive adjacencies (for the services motion).

## Buying committee

| Role | Who | What they care about | What they need from you |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Decision maker** | CEO, CTO, VP Engineering, Head of Test & Validation | Time to market, audit risk, cost of specialist headcount | Business outcome in one sentence, proof on their own data |
| **Influencer** | Project/system architects, QA leads and managers, integration leads, requirement leads, engineering managers | Consistency, traceability, less dependence on two key people who hold all the context | How output quality stays even across the team; how review effort drops |
| **Initiator / end user** | Test engineers, integration engineers, developers, project managers | Boring repetitive work, waiting on other teams, release-day scrambles | That it takes the tedious 70-90% so they do the interesting part. Never that it replaces them |
| **Gatekeeper** | Functional Safety (FuSa) Head, Quality/ASPICE lead | Can this be used in a safety process without failing an assessment? | Evidence pack early. Human-in-the-loop. No overclaiming. They can't say yes but can always say no |
| **Procurement** | Procurement leads | Vendor qualification, TISAX, contract terms | Engaged only once a deal is real. Never a campaign target |

**Rule:** a FuSa Head who distrusts you ends the deal silently. Every asset aimed at an engineering audience should survive being read by one.

## Message by persona (TestFlow example)

These are adapted from the Coda positioning pages, with the overclaims removed.

**Decision maker:** "TestFlow cuts test case creation time by 98% and writes most of your test scripts, with ASPICE and ISO 26262 attributes attached for your engineers to review. Your validation cycle shortens without adding headcount."

**Influencer:** "TestFlow standardises test cases and reports across the whole team, regardless of who wrote them, with traceability back to requirements. Your review effort drops and context stops living in two people's heads."

**Initiator:** "TestFlow gets you a 98% head start on test cases and writes up to 70% of your Python scripts. You handle the edge cases and the hardware-specific 30%. And you never hand-build a status report again."

Lines from the Coda pages worth keeping:
- "Code more, wait less." (CI/CD, developers)
- "Never write a status report again." (test reports, engineers)
- "Eliminate context bottlenecks." (CI/CD, leads)
- "Let AI write 70% of your script." (scripts, engineers)

## Buying signals

Use these to decide who to contact and to write the first line. No signal, no sequence.

**Hiring signals**
- Job posts for several test engineers, integration engineers or AUTOSAR engineers at once
- For ProcureFlow: posts for bid manager, proposal engineer, estimation engineer, technical pre-sales
- Posts for knowledge management or process improvement roles

**Program and growth signals**
- Several concurrent projects; same engineers spread across programs
- New OEM partnership or program announcements (new RFQs incoming)
- Opening a new engineering centre
- Funding rounds at automotive software companies or ESPs
- For ProcureFlow: revenue crossing roughly $20-50M, where RFQ volume outgrows a manual team

**Compliance signals**
- ASPICE Level 2 to Level 3 push (strongest single trigger - at Level 3, more processes come under assessment)
- Recently achieved ISO 26262 certification
- Hiring or posting about ISO/SAE 21434 cybersecurity work

**Behaviour signals**
- Engaging with content on test automation, ASPICE, AI in pre-sales or RFQ automation
- Attending Embedded World, AutoSens, Vector Congress, AUTOSAR Open Conference
- Using third-party integration consultants
